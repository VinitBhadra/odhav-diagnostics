Preparing for Your Visit

Tests such as Fasting Blood Sugar and Lipid Profile generally require 8–12 hours of overnight fasting (plain water is allowed). Morning hours starting at 8:00 AM are ideal for fasting samples. Call +91 73832 82266 to confirm for your specific prescription.
